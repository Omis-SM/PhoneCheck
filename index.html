PhoneCheck — Used Phone Inspector

Effective date: October 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Mohamed Darkaoui ("we," "us," or "our") provides PhoneCheck — Used Phone Inspector (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, processed, stored, and shared when you use the App, including its device diagnostics and advertising features.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described here. Where consent is required, we request it separately.

2. Information Used for Device Diagnostics

PhoneCheck provides tools to inspect and test a device's functionality. Depending on the tests you choose, the App may access or process:

  • Device and system details: device model, Android version, hardware capabilities, and relevant system characteristics.
  • Battery and charging information: battery level, charging state, and other diagnostic values made available by Android.
  • Sensors and connectivity: sensor readings, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC, mobile network, and location-related diagnostic status, where available.
  • Camera, microphone, audio, display, touch, and vibration: access needed to perform the tests you initiate.
  • Diagnostic results: test outcomes, device information, and report details generated during your use of the App.

These capabilities are used to provide the requested diagnostic functionality. Availability varies by device and Android version.

3. Permissions

Some diagnostic tests require Android permissions, such as camera, microphone, location, or nearby-device permissions. The App requests permissions when needed for a feature, subject to Android's permission controls. You can deny or revoke permissions through your device settings; affected tests may then be unavailable or incomplete.

Camera and microphone access is intended for user-initiated testing, not background surveillance.

4. Diagnostic Reports and Storage

The App can create diagnostic results and reports for your review. Report information may be stored on your device as part of the App's functionality. If you choose to export or share a report, the information in that report may be made available to the destination or recipient you select.

Please review any report before sharing it, as it may contain device characteristics and diagnostic findings. You can manage locally stored App data using available in-app options or Android's app storage controls.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertising, including banner, interstitial, and app-open ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, device information, app interactions, and ad performance data, subject to applicable settings and law.

For information about Google's data practices, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's policies.

6. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent. You may be offered choices about personalized advertising and other data processing. Where a privacy-options control is made available in the App, you can use it to review or update your choices. You may also adjust advertising-related settings on your Android device.

Declining optional advertising consent does not prevent you from using the App's core diagnostic features, although ads may still be shown where permitted.

7. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate advertising features, including Google AdMob, under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Information included in a diagnostic report may also be shared when you deliberately export or share that report.

We do not claim that third-party advertising providers avoid all data collection; their processing is governed by their own policies and your applicable choices.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored diagnostic information generally remains on your device until removed using available App functions, by clearing the App's storage, or by uninstalling the App, subject to Android behavior and any copies you have exported. Third-party advertising providers apply their own retention policies.

For questions about information associated with the App, contact us at the email address below.

9. Security

We aim to limit access to information to what is needed for the App's features and to rely on Android's platform protections. However, no method of storage or electronic processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Use care when sharing diagnostic reports.

10. Children's Privacy

PhoneCheck is intended as a device diagnostic utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not intentionally use the App to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the App, please contact us.

11. International Users

Advertising providers may process information in countries other than the one in which you reside. Their handling of information is described in their own privacy policies. Applicable privacy rights and consent requirements may vary by location.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
